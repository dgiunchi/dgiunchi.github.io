03 / A little background

From cosmic structures
to virtual worlds.

Assistant Professor in HCIHonorary Lecturer

I'm an Assistant Professor in HCI at the University of Birmingham, working on scene understanding and smart environments, and an Honorary Lecturer at University College London.

At Birmingham's VR Lab, I work with Prof. Massimiliano Di Luca and Prof. Eyal Ofek. My research spans computer graphics, human–computer interaction, machine learning, computer vision, and large language models, exploring individual and collaborative interaction in VR, AR, and XR.

Until April 2025, I was a Research Associate in UCL's Virtual Environments and Computer Graphics group. I completed my PhD at UCL in 2021, supervised by Anthony Steed and Niloy Mitra, with a European Commission Marie Curie Fellowship. During my PhD, I undertook a research internship at Microsoft and a research visit to the Max Planck Institute for Informatics.

My path into immersive computing began with astronomy at the University of Bologna and years of industry experience. I later studied optics and quantum information at Sapienza University of Rome, connecting this background with my interests in perception and digital environments.
